Clogged Sewer Line Repair in Georgetown, KY: Your Options

By the Georgetown 24/7 Plumbing team · Published October 15, 2026 · Drains and sewer

24/7 emergency line
(855) 575-2890Tap the number to call

A clogged sewer line in Georgetown, KY is usually handled in two stages: clear the blockage, then use a camera to learn why it formed. The sewer line, also called the house sewer, is the large drain pipe carrying all waste from your home to the public main. If the clog keeps returning, cleaning alone will not fix it, and the pipe itself may need repair.

This guide lays out the options in the order a plumber would consider them. Many homeowners start with drain cleaning for a single slow fixture. When several drains misbehave together, the problem is likely in the main line and the steps below apply.

Signs the clog is in the main line, not one drain

A single slow sink points to a local clog. These signs point to the sewer line.

  • Several fixtures drain slowly at the same time.
  • A toilet gurgles when the washer drains.
  • Water backs up in a floor drain or the lowest tub.
  • Sewage smell near a cleanout or in the yard.
  • Wet or unusually green patches over the line.
  • Clogs return within weeks of being cleared.

Why the camera comes before the repair decision

Clearing a clog tells you the pipe is open today. A camera tells you why it closed in the first place. Roots, a cracked joint, a sag or a buildup of grease can all produce the same backup.

Ask the plumber to clear the line first when it is fully blocked, then scan it and show you the video. Our camera inspection service is built around that sequence. The footage also gives you something to compare when a second plumber offers a different fix.

Roots and bellies: the two causes that keep coming back

Roots enter through tiny joints or cracks, looking for water and nutrients. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and grease. Cutting the roots restores flow, but unless the opening is sealed the roots return.

Not every repeat clog comes from the pipe. Grease poured down kitchen drains cools and coats the walls, and wipes labeled flushable do not break down like paper. A plumber can tell from the video whether habits or pipe damage is the cause.

A belly is a low spot where the pipe has sagged. Water pools there, solids settle and the clog forms again and again. Cleaning cannot raise a belly, so repeated clogs in the same area suggest that section may need to be replaced.

Lining versus digging

If the pipe needs repair, two broad methods exist. The camera findings decide which fits your line.

Pipe lining

  • A resin liner is cured inside the existing pipe.
  • Less excavation of the yard.
  • Needs a pipe that is still continuous.
  • Reduces the inside diameter slightly.

Dig and replace

  • Replaces the failed section with new pipe.
  • Can correct a belly or offset.
  • Needs excavation and a permit.
  • Includes restoring the dug area.

A sensible order of operations

Follow this order and you will rarely pay for the wrong repair.

  1. Stop the load

    Limit water use and avoid flushing while the line is slow or backing up.

  2. Clear the blockage

    A plumber opens the line through the cleanout with a cable or jet.

  3. Scan with a camera

    The video shows roots, cracks, sags or grease.

  4. Choose the fix

    Clean on a schedule, repair a section, line or replace, based on what the video shows.

  5. Confirm the permit

    Replacement of a house sewer needs a plumbing permit, so your plumber should arrange it.

When to call a licensed plumber

Call as soon as sewage backs up into a tub or floor drain, since wastewater in living space is a health issue. Stop using water, keep children and pets out of the area, and do not mix drain chemicals into a standing backup. Our sewer line repair page explains the work that follows a diagnosis.

Georgetown, KY local note: permits and the public sewer

Under the Kentucky plumbing regulation, 815 KAR 20:050, cleaning out waste or sewer pipes does not need a permit. Clearing a clog is maintenance, and so is repairing a leak. Putting in a new or replacement house sewer is a different matter, since the permit goes to a licensed master plumber or to a homeowner working on their own residence. If your camera shows a section must be replaced, expect the permit step before the dig.

Georgetown Municipal Water and Sewer Service, or GMWSS, provides sewer service in the city. A backup that starts at the public main is a different problem from one in your own line, so call the utility if you are unsure where the public main begins. Do not dig until Kentucky 811 has marked the buried utilities.
