Townhomes, villas and houses: who owns which pipe

In a detached house the main shutoff, the water heater and the sewer connection are yours. In a townhome or villa that shares a wall, some lines may serve more than one unit, and the association documents say who is responsible for what.

That is why the first call in an attached home is two calls. One goes to the plumbing line. The other goes to your association or property manager if water may be coming through a wall. Warranty question or plumber call?

New homes can carry a builder warranty, but a warranty does not stop water.

Check your warranty paperwork

  • A faucet that drips after only a short time in the home.
  • A fixture that was installed crooked or loose.
  • A slow drain that appeared right after move-in.

Call a plumber now

  • Water spraying or running that you cannot stop.
  • A leak that is damaging a floor or ceiling.
  • No hot water at all.
  • Sewage backing up into a drain.

Hard water reaches new fixtures too

Georgetown's utility describes its water as extremely hard and treats it with chloramine. A new home gets the same water as an old one, so scale can build up inside a new water heater over time. A tank can be drained and flushed on a schedule, and tankless water heater units need periodic descaling.

If you are choosing a new unit, water heater installation covers venting, gas or electric supply and the permit that Kentucky requires.

If water appears on a shared wall

  1. Shut off your own main

    Close the valve where the line enters your home. If you cannot find it, say so on the call.

  2. Tell your neighbor and the association

    Water in a shared wall can reach the next unit. A quick knock on the door can limit the damage.

  3. Keep clear of electricity and gas

    If water is near outlets or the panel, stay out of the wet area and switch off power only if you can reach the breaker while dry. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 from outside.

  4. Take photos before cleanup

    Pictures of the wet area help with any insurance claim and with sorting out responsibility between units.
