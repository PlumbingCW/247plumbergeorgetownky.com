Emergency work and planned work

Some pages describe jobs that cannot wait, such as a burst pipe, a sewage backup or a leaking tank. Those go to the 24/7 line, and dispatch sends a licensed plumber as soon as one is free. Other pages describe planned work, such as a new heater or a remodel, which we schedule during the day.

Each page lists cost factors rather than dollar figures, because the access, the parts and the permit decide the number. You hear a clear price before work starts.

Water heaters

A tank that leaks, rumbles or runs out of hot water usually starts with water heater repair. When the tank is beyond fixing, water heater replacement covers swapping it out, and water heater installation covers planned work. If you want endless hot water from a compact unit, a tankless water heater has its own page.

Hard water in Scott County leaves scale inside tanks, so a flush is worth requesting on any heater visit.

Drains and sewer

A slow sink or tub is a job for drain cleaning, while a whole-house backup calls for main line cleaning. When grease or roots keep returning, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall. A camera look through sewer inspection shows the cause, and sewer line repair fixes a broken or collapsed pipe.

If more than one drain is slow at once, the cause is probably downstream, which is why the main line gets its own page.

Leaks and pipes

Water spraying from a wall needs burst pipe repair right away. A hidden leak that raises your bill is found by leak detection, and a failing line from the street is handled by water service line repair. Corroded or repeatedly leaking supply pipes may be a case for repiping.

Water that keeps returning after a pipe fix is a sign to ask about the rest of the run, not only the failed section.

Fixtures

Overflowing or rocking bowls go to toilet repair and installation, and drips or low flow at the sink go to faucet repair. A jammed or humming unit under the kitchen sink is covered by garbage disposal repair.

Fixture work is often the most visible plumbing, but a failing shutoff valve under the sink is the part that turns a small repair into a flood, so ask about it during any visit.

Planned installs and ground water

New fixtures, moved lines and remodel plumbing sit under plumbing installation. For water in a lower level, sump pump installation and repair keeps the pit working, and basement waterproofing deals with seepage at its source.

Commercial

Shops, restaurants and multi-unit buildings have their own demands, and commercial plumbing describes how we handle them. Use the same 24/7 line.

Tenants and property managers should say on the call whether the building is a shop, an office or a multi-unit property, because the shutoff points and the people who must approve repairs differ. A restaurant grease line and an apartment riser are not the same job.
