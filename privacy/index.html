Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026.

Who we are

Georgetown 24/7 Plumbing operates a plumbing service line for homeowners in Georgetown, KY and nearby towns. Our address is 1031 Lexington Rd, Georgetown, KY 40324. You can reach us at (855) 575-2890 or contact@247plumbergeorgetownky.com.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, the address of the job, and what you tell us about the plumbing problem. We use that information to dispatch service, give you a clear price before work starts, and follow up on your request.

This website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

Cookies and tools

If we add analytics or call-tracking tools, they may set cookies or read basic visit data such as the page you came from. If that changes, we will update this page. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies.

How we share information

We share job details with the licensed plumber who is dispatched to your address. We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about your calls, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email contact@247plumbergeorgetownky.com and include the phone number you called from.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
